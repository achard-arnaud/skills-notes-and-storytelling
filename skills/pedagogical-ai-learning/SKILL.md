# Learning design

1. Establish audience, starting knowledge, target competence, language, accessibility and conditions of transfer. Select an explanation, demonstration or exercise by what the learner needs to do.
2. Read the [method manifest](manifest.json) and the [shared analysis contract](../../references/domain-analysis.md) for a case in this repository. Record sources and limits for factual examples. Distinguish an illustrative scenario, an observed learning outcome and a hypothesis about transfer.
3. Establish the objective, common error, task, observable success, feedback and transfer condition before choosing a learning asset. Read the [learning analysis reference](references/learning-analysis.md). Map the same learning design to a short explanation, exercise or output requested by the reader.
4. For the enterprise AI six-page landscape sheet, use the [six-page workflow](references/six-page-workflow.md) and its original content contract. Apply the [shared editorial and research review](../../references/editorial-and-research-quality.md). Assess learning and inspect the format as separate checks.

Apply the [persona review](../../references/persona-review.md) with the Head of Learning and Development lens.

## Choose the support after analysis

For a short deliverable, stay in the direct path and choose a format for the reader with the [S3 route map](../../references/s3-operations.md). Reuse the [narrative selector](../selecting-narrative-schema/SKILL.md) for decision structure when useful. Supply a scoped findings packet, not the study corpus. A complete study starts from a brief and passes the same findings to A2. In either path, route each material gap to the evidence or method owner with a reference and an exit test. Keep editorial approval with the human.
