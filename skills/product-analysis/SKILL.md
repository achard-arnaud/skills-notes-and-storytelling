# Product analysis

1. Name the product, version, audience, user job, access and target decision. Keep vendor capability, observed workflow, customer behavior and commercial traction separate.
2. Read the [method manifest](manifest.json) and the [shared analysis contract](../../references/domain-analysis.md). Select by question. Adoption calls for a defined eligible cohort, useful event, observation period and expected frequency. A capability analysis requires a user, task, workflow, dependencies and evidence of availability.
3. Bind findings to claims and retain comparative controls, counterevidence, unknowns and observation dates. Keep provider claims attributed. Analyse a missing denominator as unknown. Capture product and account facts separately until the fit question calls for their intersection.
4. Freeze the analysis before composition. For a diagnostic, benchmark or offer note, preserve the same usage/traction findings and cite their boundaries. Apply the [shared editorial and research review](../../references/editorial-and-research-quality.md).
