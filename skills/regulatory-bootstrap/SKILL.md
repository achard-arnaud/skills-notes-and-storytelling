# Regulatory analysis

1. Pin the jurisdiction, text and version, legal status, effective date, activity, actors, reader and decision or descriptive mandate. Record what changes if an exemption or transition applies.
2. Read the [method manifest](manifest.json) and the [shared analysis contract](../../references/domain-analysis.md) for a repository case. Verify official texts, distinguish authoritative law from attributed doctrine and document locators, exceptions and uncertain applicability. See [sources and analysis](references/sources-and-analysis.md).
3. For a sector mechanism, link disposition, actor, obligation, control and operational or economic effect. State a counterexample or condition that narrows the inference. Preserve legal status and evidence distinctions when reusing findings in a short note or long sector study.
4. Read the [deep regulatory workflow](references/regulatory-workflow.md) for the full research and review gates. Apply its research budget only to an in-depth regulatory run within that scope. Use the [shared editorial and research review](../../references/editorial-and-research-quality.md) for every support.

Apply the [persona review](../../references/persona-review.md) with the Risk and Compliance lens.
