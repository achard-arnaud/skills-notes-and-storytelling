# Contract performance

Use when an operational signal needs contractual interpretation or a business-value estimate. Read the actual version of the clause, applicable amendments, measurement and an independent control where available.

1. Capture the party, installation or portfolio, metric, unit, aggregation, local cut-off, comparator, threshold, effective date, exclusions and expected response. Capture the signing date separately.
2. Compare the measured value with the applicable rule for each site and period. Preserve the operational signal and its own rule separately. A signal, a contractual alert, a proven breach and a measured economic consequence require different evidence.
3. Link each conclusion to the clause, amendment and measurement claim IDs. Qualify whether the metric is observed, reported, inferred or unknown. Preserve causal unknowns and any independent control.
4. Explain the mechanism, affected user or decision, expected response, source of value, cost and attribution limits. Keep the estimate conditional when the baseline, economic exposure or counterfactual is missing.
5. Record a finding in the existing analysis envelope before mapping it to a section. Compare an affected row, a row before the effective date, an unaffected site and equality at a strict threshold. A material amendment should reopen affected findings and passages while preserving independent reviews.
