# Business analysis

1. Identify the organization, operating perimeter, decision or exploratory question, date, and audience. Establish what could materially change the business conclusion. Keep buyer reality, vendor claims and proposed fit separate.
2. Read the [method manifest](manifest.json) and the [shared analysis contract](../../references/domain-analysis.md). Select the methods that address the question. Use the case boundary for a complete study. For GreenYellow R2, use the pinned private case and its existing route.
3. Separate business model and economics, sector operations, and corporate functions. Cover data, AI, adoption and governance when they affect a decision or the economics. For contract performance, establish the applicable rule, scope, measurement and authority before qualifying the alert or estimating value.
4. Record findings, claim IDs, contradictory evidence, conditions and unknowns before choosing a template. Reuse the same result for a brief and a long report. Read the [long-form narrative](../../diagnostic-complet/skills/designing-main-story/SKILL.md) or [business brief contract](../../references/business-two-pager.md) only at composition.
5. Apply the [shared editorial and research review](../../references/editorial-and-research-quality.md). Attribute reported results and keep recommendations conditional on a decision mandate. Check the actual support at delivery.

## Choose the support after analysis

For a short deliverable, stay in the direct path and choose a format for the reader with the [S3 route map](../../references/s3-operations.md). Reuse the [narrative selector](../selecting-narrative-schema/SKILL.md) for decision structure when useful. Supply a scoped findings packet, not the study corpus. A complete study starts from a brief and passes the same findings to A2. In either path, route each material gap to the evidence or method owner with a reference and an exit test. Keep editorial approval with the human.
