# Debrief 360 operating reference

## Two coupled outputs

`debrief-360` produces two related but independently reviewable conclusions:

1. the business conclusion: what the call changed and what should happen next;
2. the coaching conclusion: which observable behaviors increased or reduced executive impact.

Do not let the coaching narrative rewrite the business evidence. Do not let a good business outcome erase a weak process, or a difficult outcome erase strong behavior.

## Delta ledger

Use four buckets:

- `confirmed`: meeting evidence supports a preparation claim;
- `contradicted`: meeting evidence materially weakens it;
- `new`: information or signal absent from preparation;
- `unknown`: important question still unresolved.

Every material update to a prior note must trace to one of these buckets.

## Posture pattern record

For each coaching claim record:

- observed fragment;
- evidence quality;
- behavior;
- likely counterparty impact;
- business consequence;
- alternative move;
- practice cue;
- confidence.

When a transcript is absent, phrase impact as an inference and avoid pseudo-precision.

## Signal and intention discipline

A signal is observable. An intention is usually inferred. Keep the chain explicit:

`observable signal → plausible interpretations → preferred hypothesis → disconfirming test`

Offer competing interpretations when the signal is ambiguous. The next question should discriminate between them.

## Sharpness lint

Remove any paragraph that does not do at least one of the following:

- prove the answer;
- qualify the answer;
- identify a decision-relevant unknown;
- prescribe a bounded next move;
- coach an observable behavior with a clear consequence.

Move useful but non-core insights into a side story or omit them.
