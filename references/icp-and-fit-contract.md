# ICP and fit contract

This contract adapts reusable elements from the `ai-maturity-diagnostic` product/fit pipeline.

## Separation of truth

Keep three states distinct:

1. **Product truth** — canonical problem, capabilities, proof, prerequisites, unknowns.
2. **Target reality** — account/partner/platform needs, architecture, constraints, timing and actors.
3. **Fit** — the explicit crossing of target reality with an immutable product snapshot.

Do not strengthen product truth from one target opportunity. Do not infer target need from product marketing.

## Seven ICP dimensions

Describe ICP conditions across seven independent dimensions:

1. **Problem** — material problem or desired outcome exists.
2. **Maturity** — readiness for the solution category.
3. **Workflow** — repeatable workflow or unit of value exists.
4. **Technical** — required systems, data, tools and controls are accessible.
5. **Organization** — sponsor, terrain owner, users and operating model are compatible.
6. **Economics** — value and full cost can be measured or reconstructed.
7. **Timing** — observable trigger and decision window.

## Anti-ICP

Define structural conditions that make the offer unsuitable or premature. Anti-ICP is a disqualification logic, not a low score.

## Hard gates

Use:
- `PASS`: evidence is sufficient;
- `OPEN`: material uncertainty remains;
- `FAIL`: condition is absent or incompatible.

Each gate records:
- severity;
- observable test;
- supporting claim/source IDs;
- consequence.

A failed blocker cannot be averaged away by scoring.

## Stakeholder lanes

When relevant distinguish:
- sponsor/promoter;
- prescriber/domain authority;
- terrain owner/user;
- technical sponsor;
- veto/control.

Titles are hypotheses, not evidence of authority.

## Alternatives and falsifiers

For every positive fit:
- compare at least one credible alternative;
- state what evidence would downgrade or invalidate the fit;
- preserve contradictions and unknowns.

## Fit decisions

Use:
- `PURSUE` — problem and major gates demonstrated, credible proof path exists;
- `VALIDATE` — plausible fit with material uncertainties to close;
- `NURTURE` — timing/ownership/readiness prevents action;
- `DISQUALIFY` — structural incompatibility or failed blocker.

## Proof vs action plan

A representative proof or validation is an analytical next step. A detailed implementation plan is a separate downstream artifact.
