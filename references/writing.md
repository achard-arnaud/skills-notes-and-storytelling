# Reader and writing profile

Resolve language, audience, purpose, tone, length, detail, allowed/forbidden terms and note style per
output. User preferences override defaults. Separate writing/layout changes from factual changes.
Business: plain language, consequence/evidence first; translate avoidable jargon in French. Technical:
precise terminology, defined abbreviations, units and assumptions. Training: familiar examples and one
new concept at a time. Preserve proper names and official method titles; explain necessary terms.
Neutral analysis is the default; vendor claims cannot become promotional certainty.

Length is a target, not permission to drop requested axes or source limitations. Split outputs or
name an annex rather than shrinking below typography floors. Keep prose in one language except
attributed quotations/proper names/approved terms. scripts/language_lint.py is a heuristic review aid,
not a reliable multilingual classifier; review findings instead of blind substitutions.
Use consistent footnotes/endnotes with claim linkage, title, author/publisher, date, locator and URL.
Physical page numbers are not article numbers. A bibliography alone does not establish claim lineage.
Translations of quotes are marked; provenance internals stay out of reader-facing action titles.
