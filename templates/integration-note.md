# Template — Integration note

## Purpose
Decide how two or more products, companies or internal capabilities should combine after a benchmark, opportunity analysis, architecture study or commercial diagnostic.

Required decision: `BUILD | BUY | PARTNER | COEXIST | DEFER`.

## Required sections
1. decision and complementarity thesis;
2. independent truth for each party/capability;
3. technical integration;
4. product/use-case integration;
5. business/GTM integration;
6. lock-in and sovereignty map by technical/value-chain layer;
7. alternative relationship models;
8. economics, ownership and operating responsibilities;
9. risks, conflicts, unknowns and falsifiers;
10. smallest reversible pilot;
11. evidence-backed bridge to the next decision.

## Technical lens
Cover interfaces, data, identity, runtime, deployment, security, observability, migration, coexistence, rollback and failure ownership.

## Product lens
Cover combined use cases, overlap, UX/workflow ownership, roadmap boundaries, onboarding, adoption, proof and cannibalization risk.

## Business lens
Cover build/buy/partner/co-distribution, ICP/channel overlap, value capture, pricing/revenue share, services, customer ownership, moat/dependency and evidenced ownership/investor/distribution links.

## Gates
A strong combined story never overrides an unresolved hard gate. Keep complementarity separate from feasibility and from commercial desirability.

## Side stories
- `comparator`: another ecosystem pattern;
- `false_lead`: apparent synergy rejected at a concrete break point;
- `analytical_focus`: integration economics, control point or lock-in mechanism;
- `dezoom`: ecosystem/portfolio implication;
- `method`: evidence boundary or untested interface.
