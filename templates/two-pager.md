# Template — Two-pager

Two-page dense executive format.

Page 1: context, entity/product/person truth, primary thesis.
Page 2: fit, implications, recommendation, next action.

Visual grammar:
- strong hierarchy;
- compact cards;
- diagrams before long prose;
- tables <=5 columns;
- semantic evidence labels;
- no body text below 9 pt.

## Couches (4-layer)

Ce template est un cas concret des [4 couches découplées](../references/layered-architecture.md).
