# Template — Opportunity note ICP

Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose
Decide whether a target account, partner, product team or internal platform is a credible opportunity for one offer.

## Boundary
Research target reality independently from product truth. Cross them only after both sides are explicit.

## Decision spine
1. Opportunity decision and why-now.
2. Target reality: strategy, architecture, operating constraints, priorities.
3. Observable capability gaps / desired outcomes.
4. Product truth: proven, plausible, planned, unknown.
5. Hard gates.
6. Gap → product capability → buyer outcome mapping.
7. Alternatives: build / buy / partner / defer.
8. Stakeholder lanes: sponsor, terrain owner, users, technical/security veto.
9. Representative proof / pilot.
10. Decision: `PURSUE | VALIDATE | NURTURE | REJECT`.

## ICP fields
- problem intensity;
- technical prerequisites;
- operating maturity;
- sovereignty/security constraints;
- economic trigger;
- timing trigger;
- anti-ICP;
- evidence required to upgrade confidence.

## Side stories
- false_lead — feature similarity without outcome fit;
- comparator — nearest credible alternative;
- analytical_focus — integration/economic mechanism;
- dezoom — strategic option value or expansion path;
- method — evidence and access limits.
