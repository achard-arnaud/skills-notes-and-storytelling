# Benchmarking candidate fixture

Checks:
- benchmark declares `technical | product | business` primary profile;
- true decision/problem is reconstructed before supplied candidate framing;
- proposed research/reranking axes have an HITL checkpoint or recorded default assumptions;
- peer discovery includes direct peers and context-relevant sovereign/European or adjacent alternatives where useful;
- unlike categories are normalized before comparison;
- hard gates precede weighted scoring;
- identical criteria and evidence status across comparable options;
- uncertainty/sensitivity visible;
- apparent winner and strongest alternative receive an adversarial/disconfirming pass;
- insufficient candidate overlap triggers clusters/fallback rather than forced ranking;
- lock-in is located in stack/value chain with exit path;
- ecosystem/complementarity remains separate from competitive ranking;
- shortlist includes fallback, reevaluation trigger and falsifiers;
- final auto-critique checks missing peers, weak claims and category bias;
- bridge reuses source/claim IDs and mandatory dreaming check executes.
