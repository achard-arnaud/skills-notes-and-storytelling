# Buy-side gap analysis candidate fixture

Decision: assess whether a challenger can replace, coexist with or complement an incumbent automation platform.

Required side stories:
- false_lead: similar trigger feature does not prove equivalent orchestration semantics;
- analytical_focus: queue migration and total operating cost;
- comparator: incumbent vs challenger vs hybrid;
- dezoom: operating-model consequences of moving from bot ownership to situation/policy ownership;
- method: vendor claims, private pricing and untested scale remain explicit limits.

Regression checks:
- comparison is a table;
- at least one hard gate is evaluated before scoring;
- migration path includes rollback;
- buyer gaps are separated from seller recommendations;
- recommendation includes a reversible validation step.
