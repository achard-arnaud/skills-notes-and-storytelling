# Debrief 360 regression fixture

Representative case: a strategic first call between an executive adviser and a technical founder after preparation notes covering product architecture, business model, competitors and partnership hypotheses. Automatic meeting notes are available; the transcript is pending.

Checks:
- canonical preparation artifacts are resolved before meeting analysis;
- delta ledger distinguishes confirmed, contradicted, new and unknown;
- answer-first opening states the business result and the single coaching priority;
- action titles express insights rather than themes;
- one primary McKinsey framework is selected because it changes the decision;
- ghost deck is written before the long-form debrief;
- business debrief and personal posture coaching remain separate;
- strengths, missed opportunities and latent signals link to meeting fragments;
- latent intentions remain hypotheses with explicit tests;
- automatic notes do not support claims about interruptions, tone, pace or speaking-time share;
- no more than three posture priorities;
- side stories include insertion and return anchors;
- next move states owner, action, metric, date and stop condition;
- confidential client claims and personal assessments are not proposed for onward sharing without authorization.
