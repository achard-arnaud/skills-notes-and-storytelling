# Architecture / strategic note regression fixture

Representative cases:
- technical platform note with event-driven architecture, business model, diagrams, evidence limits and side stories;
- business AI/Data strategy note reconstructing trajectory, organizational architecture, people, capability system, governance and strategic implications.

Checks:
- primary `analysis_dimension` is declared before scaffold and controls the decision spine;
- architecture claim supported before interpretation;
- comparison tables use density-aware widths;
- diagram transposes when scale would fall below 70%;
- reader-facing DOCX contains rendered diagram images rather than Mermaid source;
- public/private conjecture stays in analytical focus;
- direct affirmative wording preferred;
- counter-perspective challenges the main architecture choice, baseline/fallback and one hidden hard gate when material;
- a material narrowing may receive one repair and one verification pass only;
- reader/decision review keeps decision, uncertainty, falsifier and next step explicit;
- downstream benchmark/integration bridge only when a material decision remains;
- mandatory dreaming tier selection and check execute after bridge evaluation.

## Additional business-dimension checks

- strategy is reconstructed as a trajectory/capability system, not a catalogue of AI use cases;
- why-now and dated transformation signals are explicit;
- functional organization distinguishes executive sponsorship, transformation, Data/AI/Digital, IT/platform, businesses and control functions when evidence supports them;
- reconstructed organization is never presented as an official HR reporting chart without evidence;
- named leaders appear only when mandate, prior experience or public signal explains the strategy/operating model;
- business transformation separates employee augmentation, process redesign and client/product/service transformation when material;
- governance is assessed as an operating capability, including decision rights and value ownership, not only compliance;
- agentic strategy addresses identity/permissions, orchestration, observability/auditability and benefit ownership when material;
- client/growth model and strategic differentiation are connected to operating or economic consequences;
- iterative runs ledger canonical material claims and fail if compression silently removes decision value;
- preferred business diagrams include trajectory, functional operating model, capability stack and value/client bridge.
